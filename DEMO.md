# 10-minute live demo

Start the API and web app using the optional local commands in the [README](README.md). Open the dashboard and keep the green Actions run open in another tab.

| Time | On screen | What to say or do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–1:00 | Team overview | Introduce Sprintboard. All teams, issues and feedback are synthetic. Each team has 12 two-week sprints. |
| 1:00–2:00 | Team selector and metric cards | Switch from Atlas to Cedar. Compare cycle time with Atlas. Ask what the team might investigate before deciding why cycle time is longer. |
| 2:00–3:15 | Atlas sprint trends | Switch the metric from Velocity to Spillover. Open the striped bar for Sprint 06. Point out the 15 unplanned points. |
| 3:15–4:45 | Sprint 06 | Compare the actual and ideal burndown. Open **View burndown data**. Find when the remaining work rises and discuss what that says about scope. |
| 4:45–5:45 | Sprint 04 | Switch to Sprint 04. Expand the carried Atlas story. Its key and earlier timestamps show how an unfinished story crossed a sprint boundary. |
| 5:45–7:15 | Atlas Sprint 06 retrospective | Read one item in each note group. Select **Summarize notes**. Show the supporting notes and the owners of the existing actions. Explain that the summary comes from a deterministic mock. |
| 7:15–8:15 | Meeting agenda | Use Sprint 06 as the context. Switch among Planning, Review and Retrospective, and show the timeboxes. Ask which part the team would change. |
| 8:15–9:30 | GitHub Actions | Open the latest green job and its artifact. Show the hand-calculated metric tests, the labeled theme scores, and the axe and mobile reports. |
| 9:30–10:00 | Wrap-up | Ask the team to pick one metric to discuss and one change to try in the next sprint. Share the training guide. |

Direct links with the local app running are [Atlas Sprint 06](http://127.0.0.1:4173/sprints/sprint-06?team=atlas), [its retrospective](http://127.0.0.1:4173/retrospectives?team=atlas&sprint=sprint-06) and [the meeting agenda](http://127.0.0.1:4173/agenda?team=atlas&sprint=sprint-06).
